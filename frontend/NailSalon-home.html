Our Business Journey

With over 5 years of professional salon experience offering hair, nails, manicures, pedicures, facials, and massage services. Elaine Navarro Reyes launched her home-service business in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Starting from local community and Barangay Group Chats (GCs) with simple and transparent pricelists, PinkieBabe Nails has grown through strong client loyalty and word-of-mouth recommendations built on trusted, top-quality care right at your doorstep.
